## Method

1. **Proof:** Take the dough out of the fridge 1–2 hours before service and let it reach room temperature.
2. **Heat the oven:** Deck oven at 500–550°F (260–290°C). Conveyor oven: 470°F for about 6 minutes.
3. **Stretch:** Hand-stretch the dough to 14", keeping a ¾" rim for the crust.
4. **Sauce:** Spread 3 oz of buffalo sauce evenly, stopping ¾" from the edge. No ranch anywhere on this pizza.
5. **Toppings:** Spread 5 oz of mozzarella evenly.
6. **Toppings:** Scatter 4 oz of crispy chicken evenly over the cheese.
7. **Pineapple:** Pat the chunks dry and scatter 2 oz evenly. Dry fruit keeps the pizza from getting soggy, and its sweetness balances the buffalo heat.
8. **Bake:** 8–12 minutes in a deck oven, until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbling.
9. **Finish:** Scatter 1 oz of blue cheese crumbles and 0.5 oz of sliced celery over the hot pizza, then cut into 8 slices.

## Quality check
- Chicken is crispy on top, not soggy
- Buffalo sauce is bubbling, not burnt at the edges
- Blue cheese is added after baking, so it is soft but not melted away
- Celery is fresh and crunchy
- No pools of liquid from the pineapple
