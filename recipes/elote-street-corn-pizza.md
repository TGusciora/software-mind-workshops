## Method

1. **Proof:** Take the dough out of the fridge 1–2 hours before service and let it reach room temperature.
2. **Heat the oven:** Deck oven at 500–550°F (260–290°C). Conveyor oven: 470°F for about 6 minutes.
3. **Stretch:** Hand-stretch the dough to 14", keeping a ¾" rim for the crust.
4. **Sauce:** Spread the chipotle crema base evenly, stopping ¾" from the edge.
5. **Cheese:** Spread 7 oz of the cheese evenly.
6. **Toppings:** Scatter 3 oz of charred corn and 1 oz of pickled red onion.
7. **Pineapple:** Pat the chunks dry and scatter 2 oz evenly. Dry fruit keeps the pizza from getting soggy.
8. **Bake:** 8–12 minutes in a deck oven, until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbling.
9. **Finish:** Add the finishing ingredients, then cut into 8 slices.

## Quality check
- Cotija, lime and Tajín go on after baking
- Corn is charred, not steamed
- No pools of liquid from the pineapple
