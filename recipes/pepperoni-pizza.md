## Method

1. **Proof:** Take the dough out of the fridge 1–2 hours before service and let it reach room temperature.
2. **Heat the oven:** Deck oven at 500–550°F (260–290°C). Conveyor oven: 470°F for about 6 minutes.
3. **Stretch:** Hand-stretch the dough to 14", keeping a ¾" rim for the crust.
4. **Sauce:** Spread 3 oz of sauce in a spiral, stopping ¾" from the edge.
5. **Cheese:** Spread 7 oz of mozzarella evenly all the way to the sauce line.
6. **Pineapple:** Pat the chunks dry and scatter 2 oz evenly. Dry fruit keeps the pizza from getting soggy.
7. **Pepperoni:** Lay about 40 slices in rings from the edge toward the center, overlapping slightly.
8. **Bake:** 8–12 minutes in a deck oven, until the crust is golden brown, the cheese is bubbling, and the pepperoni cups and chars.
9. **Finish:** Brush the crust with olive oil, then sprinkle with oregano and parmesan. Cut into 8 slices.

## Quality check
- Pepperoni is cupped, with crisp edges
- Bottom is spotted brown, not pale or burnt
- No pools of liquid from the pineapple
