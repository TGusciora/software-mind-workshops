## Method

1. **Proof:** Take the dough out of the fridge 1–2 hours before service and let it reach room temperature.
2. **Toast the pecans:** Toast 1.5 oz of halved pecans at 350°F for 6–8 minutes until fragrant. Cool before use.
3. **Heat the oven:** Deck oven at 475–500°F (245–260°C). Conveyor oven: 450°F for about 5 minutes.
4. **Stretch:** Hand-stretch the dough to 10", keeping a ½" rim for the crust.
5. **Base:** Brush 1.5 oz of cinnamon-butter over the dough, stopping ½" from the edge.
6. **Cream layer:** Blend 3 oz of mascarpone with 2 oz of cream cheese and dollop evenly over the base.
7. **Pineapple:** Pat the chunks dry and scatter 2 oz evenly. Dry fruit keeps the pizza from getting soggy.
8. **Bake:** 6–9 minutes in a deck oven, until the crust is golden and the cream layer is just set and lightly blistered.
9. **Finish:** Straight out of the oven, scatter the toasted pecans, drizzle with 1 oz of caramel, dust with powdered sugar, then cut into 6 slices.

## Quality check
- Crust is golden with a caramelized cinnamon-butter edge, not burnt
- Cream layer is smooth and warm, not split or oily
- Pecans are crisp and toasted, added after the bake so they stay crunchy
- No pools of liquid from the pineapple
