## Method

1. **Proof:** Take the dough out of the fridge 1–2 hours before service and let it reach room temperature.
2. **Heat the oven:** Deck oven at 500–550°F (260–290°C). Conveyor oven: 470°F for about 6 minutes.
3. **Season the shrimp:** Toss 5 oz of shrimp with the Cajun seasoning; pat dry.
4. **Stretch:** Hand-stretch the dough to 14", keeping a ¾" rim for the crust.
5. **Sauce:** Spread the garlic-butter sauce evenly, stopping ¾" from the edge.
6. **Toppings:** Spread 5 oz of mozzarella evenly.
7. **Toppings:** Scatter the seasoned shrimp and 1.5 oz of bell pepper evenly.
8. **Pineapple:** Pat the chunks dry and scatter 2 oz evenly. Dry fruit keeps the pizza from getting soggy.
9. **Bake:** 8–12 minutes in a deck oven, until the crust is golden brown and the shrimp are pink and just cooked through.
10. **Finish:** Squeeze lemon over the top, add parsley, then cut into 8 slices.

## Quality check
- Shrimp are pink and tender, not rubbery
- Garlic butter is not burnt at the edges
- No pools of liquid from the pineapple
- Lemon finish is bright, not overpowering
