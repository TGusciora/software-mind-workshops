## Method

1. **Prep meats (before service):** Par-cook the bacon until it's about 70% done, then chop it. Brown the ground beef with salt, pepper, and garlic powder, then drain it well. Dice the ham. Keep all prepped meats cold.
2. **Proof:** Take the dough out of the fridge 1–2 hours before service and let it reach room temperature.
3. **Heat the oven:** Deck oven at 500–550°F (260–290°C). Conveyor oven: 470°F for about 6½ minutes (the heavier load needs a little longer).
4. **Stretch:** Hand-stretch the dough to 14", keeping a ¾" rim for the crust.
5. **Sauce:** Spread 3 oz of sauce in a spiral, stopping ¾" from the edge.
6. **Cheese:** Spread 7 oz of mozzarella evenly all the way to the sauce line.
7. **Ground meats:** Scatter the raw sausage pieces and the ground beef evenly. Keep the sausage pieces small (about ½") so they cook all the way through.
8. **Ham & bacon:** Scatter the ham and bacon evenly.
9. **Pepperoni:** Put the pepperoni on last, about 24 slices, so it cups and chars.
10. **Bake:** 10–13 minutes in a deck oven, until the crust is golden brown, the cheese is bubbling, and the sausage reaches 160°F inside.
11. **Rest & finish:** Rest for 1 minute so the grease settles. Blot any pools of grease, brush the crust with olive oil, then sprinkle with oregano and parmesan. Cut into 8 slices.

## Quality check
- Sausage is fully cooked (160°F), with no pink centers
- Pepperoni is cupped, with crisp edges; bacon is crisp, not chewy
- Meat covers the pizza evenly, with no bare patches and no piles
- Bottom is spotted brown and stays crisp under the heavy toppings
- No pools of grease
