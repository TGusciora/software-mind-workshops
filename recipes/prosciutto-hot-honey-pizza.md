## Method

1. **Proof:** Take the dough out of the fridge 1–2 hours before service and let it reach room temperature.
2. **Heat the oven:** Deck oven at 500–550°F (260–290°C). Conveyor oven: 470°F for about 6 minutes.
3. **Stretch:** Hand-stretch the dough to 14", keeping a ¾" rim for the crust.
4. **Base:** Brush 1.5 oz of garlic-infused olive oil and roasted garlic evenly, stopping ¾" from the edge.
5. **Toppings:** Spread 6 oz of mozzarella evenly.
6. **Pineapple:** Pat the chunks dry and scatter 2 oz evenly. Dry fruit keeps the pizza from getting soggy.
7. **Bake:** 8–12 minutes in a deck oven, until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbling.
8. **Finish:** Straight out of the oven, drape 2.5 oz of prosciutto over the pizza so it warms without crisping. Top with 1 oz arugula, drizzle 0.5 oz hot honey, and add 0.5 oz shaved parmesan, then cut into 8 slices.

## Quality check
- Prosciutto is silky and pink, not dried out or crisp
- Arugula is fresh and only lightly wilted
- Hot honey is a thin drizzle, not pooled
- No pools of liquid from the pineapple
