## Method

1. **Marinate:** Marinate the skirt steak at least 4 hours (up to 24) in lime juice, cumin, garlic and chili. Grill hot, rest, then slice thin against the grain.
2. **Proof:** Take the dough out of the fridge 1–2 hours before service and let it reach room temperature.
3. **Heat the oven:** Deck oven at 500–550°F (260–290°C). Conveyor oven: 470°F for about 6 minutes.
4. **Stretch:** Hand-stretch the dough to 14", keeping a ¾" rim for the crust.
5. **Base:** Spread 3 oz of queso base evenly, stopping ¾" from the edge.
6. **Cheese:** Spread 5 oz of Monterey Jack evenly.
7. **Toppings:** Scatter 4 oz of steak, 1.5 oz of peppers and 1 oz of red onion evenly.
8. **Pineapple:** Pat the chunks dry and scatter 2 oz evenly. Dry fruit keeps the pizza from getting soggy.
9. **Bake:** 8–12 minutes in a deck oven, until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbling.
10. **Finish:** Drizzle 1.5 oz of lime crema, add cilantro, then cut into 8 slices.

## Quality check
- Steak is tender and juicy, sliced thin, not gray or chewy
- Peppers and onions are lightly charred, not raw or burnt
- Queso and Jack are bubbling, with no oil separation
- No pools of liquid from the pineapple
- Lime crema is added after the bake, so it stays cool and creamy
