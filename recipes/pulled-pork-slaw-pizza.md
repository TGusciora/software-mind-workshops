## Method

1. **Proof:** Take the dough out of the fridge 1–2 hours before service and let it reach room temperature.
2. **Heat the oven:** Deck oven at 500–550°F (260–290°C). Conveyor oven: 470°F for about 6 minutes.
3. **Stretch:** Hand-stretch the dough to 14", keeping a ¾" rim for the crust.
4. **Sauce:** Spread 3 oz of the vinegar BBQ sauce evenly, stopping ¾" from the edge.
5. **Cheese:** Toss the mozzarella and cheddar together and spread the 6 oz blend evenly.
6. **Toppings:** Scatter 4 oz of pulled pork in small clumps, so it stays juicy and does not dry out.
7. **Pineapple:** Pat the chunks dry and scatter 2 oz evenly. Dry fruit keeps the pizza from getting soggy.
8. **Bake:** 8–12 minutes in a deck oven, until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbling.
9. **Finish:** Out of the oven, pile 2 oz of cold slaw in the center, add the pickled onion and cilantro, then cut into 8 slices. Never bake the slaw.

## Quality check
- Pulled pork is hot and moist, with browned edges, not dried out
- Slaw is cold and crunchy, added only after baking
- BBQ sauce is tangy and bubbling, not burnt at the edges
- No pools of liquid from the pineapple
