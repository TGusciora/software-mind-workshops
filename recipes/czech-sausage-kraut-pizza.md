## Method

1. **Proof:** Take the dough out of the fridge 1–2 hours before service and let it reach room temperature.
2. **Heat the oven:** Deck oven at 500–550°F (260–290°C). Conveyor oven: 470°F for about 6 minutes.
3. **Stretch:** Hand-stretch the dough to 14", keeping a ¾" rim for the crust.
4. **Base:** Spread 3 oz of mustard-cream evenly, stopping ¾" from the edge.
5. **Toppings:** Spread 6 oz of the Swiss/mozzarella blend evenly.
6. **Toppings:** Scatter 4 oz of sausage coins and 2 oz of well-drained sauerkraut evenly.
7. **Pineapple:** Pat the chunks dry and scatter 2 oz evenly. Dry fruit keeps the pizza from getting soggy.
8. **Bake:** 8–12 minutes in a deck oven, until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbling.
9. **Finish:** Sprinkle with caraway seeds and parsley, then cut into 8 slices.

## Quality check
- Sausage coins are hot and lightly crisped at the edges
- Sauerkraut is well drained, with no pools of liquid
- Mustard-cream is set, not split or greasy
- Pineapple is dry and evenly distributed
- Caraway is present but not overpowering
