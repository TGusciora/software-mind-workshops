## Method

1. **Proof:** Take the dough out of the fridge 1–2 hours before service and let it reach room temperature.
2. **Heat the oven:** Deck oven at 500–550°F (260–290°C). Conveyor oven: 470°F for about 6 minutes.
3. **Prep:** Cook the sausage and potato ahead. Soft-scramble the eggs until barely set (they finish cooking in the oven).
4. **Stretch:** Hand-stretch the dough to 14", keeping a ¾" rim for the crust.
5. **Base:** Spread 3 oz of sausage gravy (or salsa roja) thinly, stopping ¾" from the edge.
6. **Toppings:** Spread 6 oz of the cheddar and Jack blend evenly.
7. **Toppings:** Scatter 3 oz of sausage and 2 oz of potato evenly.
8. **Pineapple:** Pat the chunks dry and scatter 2 oz evenly. Dry fruit keeps the pizza from getting soggy.
9. **Bake:** 8–12 minutes in a deck oven, until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbling.
10. **Finish:** Add the scrambled eggs on top for the last minute of baking (or right after), then top with pico, cilantro and hot sauce and cut into 8 slices.

## Quality check
- Eggs are soft and fluffy, not rubbery or browned
- Gravy is thin and even, not pooled or gummy
- Potato is crisp, not soggy
- No pools of liquid from the pineapple
