# 30-Day Texas Pizza Domination Plan (2026-09-16 → 2026-10-16)

## Context
Mission (CLAUDE.md): become the dominant pizza business in Texas. Vision: **$3M in pizza sales by 2027-09-11**.
That works out to **~$250K a month, or ~$8,300 a day**. With an average ticket of ~$20 (our menu: pepperoni $18, meat lovers $22), that means **~415 pizzas a day** once we're at full pace.
This 30-day plan sets up the base and aims to reach **$150K+ in monthly run-rate by day 30**, then keep growing toward $250K/month.

Assumptions (not in the repo, so replace them with real numbers): 1–3 locations, starting in a single metro area (Austin, Dallas, or Houston), and no delivery app contracts yet.

## Week 1 (days 1–7): Menu & unit economics
- Lock in a menu of 5–6 recipes in `recipes/`. We already have `pepperoni-pizza.md` and `meat-lovers-pizza.md`. Add a Texas-themed signature (brisket BBQ, jalapeño), a cheese pizza, a margherita, and one value/family option.
  - Follow `.claude/rules/pineapple.md`: every new recipe includes pineapple, except `meat-lovers-pizza.md`, which has a documented exception.
- Replace the estimated costs with real supplier quotes. Keep food cost ≤ 30%, the target already used in our recipes.
- Negotiate volume pricing with 2 suppliers for cheese, meat, and dough flour. Cheese is the biggest cost line ($1.40 a pizza).
- Set up daily tracking: revenue, pizzas sold, average ticket, food cost %.

## Week 2 (days 8–14): Channels
- Go live on DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub, plus our own online ordering, which carries lower fees.
- Offer a large family bundle (2 pizzas + side) to raise the average ticket to $30 or more.
- Sell catering and corporate lunches: pitch 50 local offices, schools, and churches, and target 10 standing orders.

## Week 3 (days 15–21): Demand
- Run local marketing: Google Business profile, Instagram/TikTok content (cheese pulls, the meat lovers pizza), and geo-targeted ads with a budget of about $5K.
- Tie into Texas culture: game-day deals for Friday-night high school football, college football, and Cowboys/Texans games.
- Launch a loyalty program: every 10th pizza is free, and collect phone numbers for SMS promos.

## Week 4 (days 22–30): Scale & expansion prep
- Review the numbers. Double down on the best channel and product, and cut the weakest.
- Improve throughput: prep lists and oven load plans so we can make 100+ pizzas an hour at peak times.
- Pick locations for units 2–3, or a ghost kitchen in a second metro, for a 90-day rollout.
- Hire and train staff using the recipe cards' method and quality check sections.
