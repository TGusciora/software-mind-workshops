# Questionnaire: customer feedback breakdown

The request was "break down a customer feedback thing for us". No input files were provided and the user was not available, so every question below was answered with an assumption. The assumptions come from the repo context: CLAUDE.md, `plans/30-day-plan.md`, `plans/180-day-plan.md`, and `pizza-creator/`. Change any answer and the breakdown in `customer-feedback-user-stories.md` should change with it.

| # | Question | Assumed answer | Why I assumed this |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the "customer feedback thing"? Is it a feature to build, a pile of existing feedback to analyse, or a process? | **A product feature** that collects customer feedback after each order and helps us act on it. | The request says "break down", and the repo builds software (Pizza Creator, the store map). No feedback data was attached to analyse. |
| 2 | What kind of breakdown do you want? | **User stories**: personas, epics, "As a... I want... so that..." stories with Given/When/Then acceptance criteria and MoSCoW priorities, ready to feed into a spec. | This is the usual first step before a spec. `docs/specs/` already exists for the next step. |
| 3 | Who gives the feedback? | Customers who order through **our own online ordering**, walk-in/pickup customers with a phone number on file (loyalty), and **catering accounts**. | The 30-day plan launches own online ordering, SMS loyalty, and catering. |
| 4 | Do we include DoorDash / Uber Eats / Grubhub customers? | **Not in v1.** We cannot message app customers directly. Pulling in app ratings is a "Could". | Delivery apps own that customer contact. |
| 5 | How do we reach customers? | **SMS first**, with email as a fallback. A link opens a short mobile web form. | The loyalty program collects phone numbers for SMS promos. |
| 6 | What do we ask? | A 1–5 star overall rating, then optional per-pizza thumbs up/down, optional tags (late, cold, wrong order, great taste, and so on), and an optional comment. It must take under 30 seconds. | Short surveys get more responses. Per-pizza ratings support the "cut the weakest" menu review. |
| 7 | What happens with bad feedback? | A rating of 1–2 stars **alerts the store manager**, who must contact the customer within a set time and can issue a make-good credit or coupon. | Winning back unhappy customers protects repeat revenue, which the $3M target depends on. |
| 8 | What happens with good feedback? | A rating of 4–5 stars prompts the customer to **leave a Google review** for that store. | The 30-day plan relies on the Google Business profile for local demand. We will not filter review prompts by sentiment in a way that breaks Google policy (see open questions). |
| 9 | Who looks at the results? | Store managers (their own store), the owner/ops lead (all stores), and marketing. | We go from 1–3 units to 7 units across 3 metros in 180 days. We need a way to compare stores. |
| 10 | Which systems does it connect to? | Our own ordering system (order ID, items, store, channel), the loyalty customer record (phone number, consent), and an SMS provider. Tool choice goes in the spec, not here. | This keeps the stories independent of any tech choice. |
| 11 | Deadline or budget? | Nothing given. I assumed an **MVP within about 4 weeks** so it is live before the holiday push (days 31–60 of the 180-day plan). | Feedback is most useful during high-volume periods and while new units are opening. |
| 12 | Privacy / legal constraints? | Send SMS only to customers who opted in (TCPA). Support opt-out with STOP. Store comments linked to the customer, visible only to staff. | This is the standard baseline for US SMS marketing. |
| 13 | Is the Pizza Creator in scope? | **Could.** Ratings on custom pizzas could show which topping combinations to add to the menu. | Pizza Creator exists but has no checkout yet, so there are no orders to rate. |
| 14 | How do we measure success? | Response rate ≥ 15%. Average rating ≥ 4.3. 90% of 1–2 star ratings contacted within 24h. Google reviews per store +50/month. Repeat rate of recovered customers ≥ the repeat rate of neutral customers. | These tie the feature to repeat orders and local discovery, which means revenue. |
